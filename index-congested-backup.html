Questions Indians actually ask

GST, PAN, refunds — answered without “ecosystem” fluff. Still stuck? support@innfill.in replies in 24–48h.

Need invoice with GST?
We provide GST invoices for the 14% fee. Your main service amount is between you and freelancer (consult your CA for TDS).
Why 14% and not “5–10%”?

Because 5% can’t fund escrow, disputes, and bank payouts. Our terms §6.2 have always been 14% + 18% GST on that fee. We fixed the homepage lie — this is the real math. Compare on ₹10k: you pay 10,252, freelancer gets 8,600.

Is my money safe? What if freelancer disappears?

Razorpay escrow holds funds. If no delivery in agreed time, raise dispute; we mediate with chat + files. Decision in 7 days. If you vanish 7 days after delivery, order auto-completes to protect freelancer — check your email.

Do you store my card? What PAN/IFSC do you need?

Never. Razorpay handles cards/UPI. We store only Razorpay payment IDs. Freelancers add account number + IFSC + PAN for payouts (tax compliance) — stored in Supabase with Row Level Security. See Privacy §5.2.

When do I get paid? Minimum?

1–3 business days after client approves, directly to bank. Minimum ₹100. No wallet fees.

How is Innfill different from Fiverr/Upwork for an Indian startup?

Fiverr/Upwork charge 20% + FX + withdrawal fees, US dispute hours, and “AI gig” spam. We’re Hyderabad-based, 14%, INR-first (UPI/NetBanking), and community-led — we know what a ₹25k landing means to a second-year student founder.
